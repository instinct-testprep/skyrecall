Support

Questions, a problem with a purchase, or a data request? Email d@gorbunov.me. Include your iPhone model, iOS version and a short description. We read every message.

Restore a purchase

Open the app, go to the Unlock screen and tap Restore Purchases. You need to be signed in to the same Apple Account that bought it, and online.

About

SkyRecall is an independent study aid for the FAA Part 107 remote pilot knowledge test. It is not the real test, does not promise a passing score, and is not produced, endorsed or approved by the FAA. Check current FAA material before your exam or any flight.
