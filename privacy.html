SkyRecall Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026. Publisher: Dmitrii Gorbunov. Contact: d@gorbunov.me.

This policy covers the SkyRecall: Part 107 Prep iPhone app.

Study data stays on your device

The app saves your practice progress, answer history, exam history, language, goals and reminder settings in local app storage on your device. The app does not send these study records to a server run by us. Your device backup or transfer service (for example iCloud) may copy this data under your Apple settings. There is no account or sign-in.

Purchases

The app offers a free set of sample questions and one optional one-time purchase that unlocks the full question bank. Purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check that a purchase is valid and to unlock the full bank. RevenueCat assigns your installation a random anonymous identifier and receives your purchase history for this app and basic device and app information needed to do this. We do not collect your name, email address or payment card details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy for how they handle data.

Reminders and links

If you turn on study reminders, they are scheduled on your device by iOS after you give permission. We do not run a push notification server. If you open an external link, that website handles your visit under its own policy.

Advertising and tracking

The app has no ads, no advertising identifier use and no third-party analytics SDK. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How long data is kept

  • Study data: stays on your device until you delete the app. Backups made by Apple follow Apple's rules.
  • Purchase record: the anonymous purchase record at RevenueCat, and the purchase record at Apple, are kept until you ask us to delete the RevenueCat record, or as Apple's or RevenueCat's own legal and accounting obligations require.

Deleting your data

To delete study data, delete the app. To ask for deletion of your anonymous purchase record, email d@gorbunov.me. Because the record is not tied to your name or email, we may need an identifier from you, such as your Apple order or transaction number, to find it. We will tell you what we could and could not delete. Apple keeps its own records, which we cannot delete.

Children

SkyRecall is intended for people 13 and older. The FAA requires remote pilots to be at least 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has used the app, email us and we will delete any purchase record we can locate.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU), you may have rights to access or delete personal data. Email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above on this page.

Not affiliated with the FAA

SkyRecall is an independent study aid and is not produced, endorsed or approved by the FAA.
